# Buddy & the Big Wide Window

Buddy's published story is a three-part Saturday morning newspaper comic. Read the episodes in order:

1. [Someone Out There](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-1.pdf) — Buddy discovers that someone is watching from the other side of the window.
2. [The Wobbly Gyro](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-2.pdf) — A spinning gyro turns his first game into an adventure.
3. [The Five-Block Mystery](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-3.pdf) — Buddy takes on a new puzzle with five colorful blocks.

The full-page artwork is also available for [Episode 1](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-1.png), [Episode 2](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-2.png), and [Episode 3](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-3.png). Earlier comic concepts are preserved in the [project archive](archive/comics/README.md) and are not part of the published story.
