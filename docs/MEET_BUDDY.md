# Meet Buddy

Buddy is the little virtual companion at the heart of R.O.B. Vision. In our story, he has spent years inside an emulator, hearing instructions as flashes of light but never seeing who is on the other side. The game-frame link gives those signals a new destination. His first greeting is a blink on the UNO Q; his next is a movement on your screen.

![Buddy carrying a glowing block as pixels trail from his base](images/buddy-concept-v1.png)

Buddy is curious, patient, and happiest when you give him something to do. Watch his eyes, hands, and game pieces as each received command becomes a visible action. When the game ends, he waits for the next round.

This illustration is Buddy's story portrait. Mission shows the working virtual model and its exact game state; use the on-screen model and status indicators when following setup or gameplay steps. Buddy is an original character concept for this independent maker project. Nintendo's games and historical robot remain their owners' work.
