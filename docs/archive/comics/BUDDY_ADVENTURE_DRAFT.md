# Buddy & the Big Wide Window

**Three Saturday morning newspaper adventures.** Read [Episode 1: Someone Out There](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-1.pdf), [Episode 2: The Wobbly Gyro](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-2.pdf), and [Episode 3: The Five-Block Mystery](../output/pdf/Buddy-Big-Wide-Window-Episode-3.pdf). Their artwork is available for [Episode 1](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-1.png), [Episode 2](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-2.png), and [Episode 3](images/buddy-newspaper-episode-3.png).

Buddy knows what to do when the game flashes, but he has never met the person on the other side. One unexpected blink gives him a window into their room. His first morning there brings a spinning gyro, a tower of five blocks, and a moment when the signal goes quiet. Buddy discovers that he can hold on until his friend returns.

## Episode script

### 1. The other side

1. Buddy peeks around the edge of a dark game frame. **Buddy, thinking:** “Turn. Lift. Wait. I know every signal. But who keeps sending them?”
2. A small grid lights up on a nearby desk. **Buddy:** “Huh? That blink was not mine. ...Hello?”
3. The lights become a window onto a much bigger world. **Buddy:** “A window! Wait. Those are my hands!”
4. He lifts a glowing block and realizes someone can see him. **Buddy:** “A table! A block! Okay, Buddy. Look cool. Set. It. Down.”

### 2. The spinny thing

1. A gyro waits beside red and blue pads. **Buddy:** “Good morning, world! First mission: one gyro, two pads, zero panicking.”
2. He lifts the gyro between both hands. **Buddy, thinking:** “Easy... easy... Why does the tiny tornado have a point?”
3. The gyro spins on the blue pad, and a little adventurer passes a blue gate. **Buddy:** “Blue gate, open up! Go on, little hero - I have got you!”
4. The gyro wobbles and tips. Buddy takes it back to its holder. **Buddy:** “Oops! Even a top needs a rest. Back to the holder, you.”

### 3. Five blocks and a feeling

1. Five bright blocks wait in a tower. **Buddy:** “Five blocks. One tower. I was programmed for this! ...I think.”
2. He reaches around the stack with both hands. **Buddy, thinking:** “Hands wide. Down. Close. Breathe. Wait - do I breathe?”
3. He carries the tower toward another tray. **Buddy:** “I have got them! Nobody sneeze!”
4. The game signal disappears. Buddy freezes, still holding the blocks. **Buddy:** “The signal is gone. I am still holding on... Are you still there?”

### 4. The next round

1. The little grid lights up again. **Buddy:** “There you are! For a second I thought the window had closed.”
2. He settles the stack in its tray and opens both hands. **Buddy:** “Steady... open hands... ta-da!”
3. The player gives him a thumbs-up. **Player:** “Nice save!” **Buddy:** “I knew you were there!”
4. Buddy watches his new friend eat breakfast from inside the browser window. **Buddy, thinking:** “Maybe I never needed to leave the game. I just needed a friend on the other side.” **Buddy:** “...Do robots get pancakes?”

Buddy's adventure takes place in the virtual browser scene; the human's room is on the other side of his window.
